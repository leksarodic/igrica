# Audio Direction — *The Long Note*

**Statement.** The valley is an instrument. The score is not a soundtrack laid over it; it is the sound of the place being played. Most of the time, that means restraint: wind, water, reeds, footsteps, a distant bell, and a few slow tones. Music arrives when the player discovers something or when people gather.

All audio in this build is original and synthesized in-engine. No external music or recordings are shipped.

---

## 1. Sound identity

- **Palette:** struck stone bells, plucked gut/reed strings, low wooden flute, breath, water, dry reed rustle, copper rings, felt hammers, soft choir-like vowel pads (synthesized, wordless).
- **Tuning:** modal. Bellhollow and Reedwater use a warm Dorian; the Cantor ruins use a hollow Aeolian with open fifths; Glasswood uses a sparse, detuned Lydian shimmer; the Hush removes the fundamental and leaves only air and a faint upper partial.
- **Tempo is not a metronome.** Rhythms emerge from the environment (water, footsteps, work) and from sparse bell patterns. Combat adds a low pulse but never a driving loop.
- **Silence is a colour.** The Hush is defined by the score thinning out, not by a scary drone. After a big discovery, the game often goes quiet for several seconds.

---

## 2. Music system

`MusicDirector` (procedural, no audio files) runs a layered generative score:

| Layer | Content | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| **Bed** | Slow harmonic pad, 20–40 s swells, root/fifth/third | Always, at low level, unless Hush |
| **Surface** | Sparse plucked/bell motif from a seeded phrase library | Exploration, every 20–60 s |
| **Working** | Rhythmic felt/wood pattern, human and small | Hub, crafting, building |
| **Gathering** | Add a low flute line and a second voice | Town events, companion scenes |
| **Tension** | Low pulse, reduced harmonic movement, no melody | Combat, danger |
| **Boss** | Open-fifth choir pad + a repeating three-note figure tied to the boss's phase | Boss arenas |
| **Hush** | Filtered air, no fundamental, occasional reversed bell tail | Hush proximity |

Transitions are crossfades (2–6 s) on bar boundaries; no stingers on pickups or quest completions. A "music off" setting leaves ambience intact. The director never uses tempo or volume to create urgency or pressure.

**Seeded composition.** Each save generates a musical seed. Melodic phrases are drawn from that seed, so a player's valley has a consistent melodic identity across sessions and devices (the seed is stored in the save). The seed changes only on New Game.

---

## 3. Ambience

Layered, region-specific, with randomized one-shots and distance filtering:

- **Bellhollow:** wind over roofs, copper creak, distant conversation murmur (wordless), kettle-birds, a workshop hammer, water in the basin, a bell every few minutes.
- **Reedwater:** water lapping, reed rustle, herons, insect ticks, a ferry rope, far-off reed flutes.
- **Kestrel Crags:** high wind, rope creak, hawk calls, stone ticks, quarry echoes.
- **Glasswood:** near-silence, a glassy shimmer when the wind moves, a single sustained tone.
- **Undersong:** deep room tone, water drops with long tails, resonant hum that changes with the player's footsteps, chime columns that ring when brushed.
- **The Hush:** air with no reflections, muffled footsteps, a faint high sine, captions note the absence.

Implementation: pre-baked stereo loops (30–60 s, seamless) generated by the synth at startup and cached, plus pooled one-shots with randomized pitch/volume and 2D/3D attenuation. Ambience ducks under dialogue by 4 dB, never to zero.

---

## 4. Interaction and combat audio

Every action has a short, distinct, non-gratuitous sound:

| Event | Sound |
|---|---|
| Footstep | Soft granular thud, material-dependent (stone/wood/water/reed) |
| Light attack | Air swish + a short plucked tone; hit = felt thump + bell partial |
| Heavy attack | Lower whoosh, longer stone/copper impact |
| Parry | Bright metallic tick + a clean fifth |
| Dodge | Reed rustle + breath |
| Listen pulse | A low bell inhale, then a wide, soft return ping |
| Pickup | Single soft chime, pitch based on item class (never a fanfare) |
| Craft | Felt hammer, copper ring, a small rise |
| Build | Wood settle, stone thunk, a satisfied low note |
| Puzzle correct | Held chord that resolves; wrong = a dry, neutral click |
| Save | One copper bell, quiet |
| Low health | Heartbeat-like low pulse, fading in; no alarm |
| Enemy defeat | Motes + a resolving chord, no gore |
| Companion ability | A distinctive two-note motif per companion |

**Pitch language in audio:** Low = 110–165 Hz, Mid = 220–330 Hz, High = 440–660 Hz. Each pitch has a timbre: Low is wooden/stone, Mid is gut/reed, High is glass/bell. This mirrors the visual marks and makes the system learnable by ear.

---

## 5. Voice and dialogue

No recorded voice acting in this build. Dialogue is text with speaker-specific "voice tones": a short synthesized motif when a character begins speaking (Sable = low bell; Nissa = plucked reed; Brannoc = wooden knock; Pell = paper rustle and a high tick; the Choirmaster = a chord with the fundamental missing). These are quiet, non-verbal, and can be disabled. Future voice acting would follow the same casting notes in `docs/story/characters.md`.

---

## 6. Mixing

- Buses: Master → Music / Ambience / SFX / UI / DialogueTone. Master has a gentle limiter (−1 dBFS ceiling). No compression pumping.
- Target loudness: −16 LUFS integrated for the mix, with peaks below −3 dBFS; dialogue/text is not audio-gated.
- Music sits 6–10 dB under ambience in exploration, rises to −3 dB under it in boss fights, and drops 4 dB under dialogue.
- 3D attenuation: linear-ish with a 2 m near field and 25 m far field; occlusion via a simple raycast low-pass (Mac); disabled on iPhone Low.
- Mono downmix option; separate sliders; a "test sound" button in settings.
- The game starts at 70% master. No audio autoplay on the title screen until the player presses a key (platform courtesy and iOS audio-session correctness).

---

## 7. Implementation notes

- `Synth` generates `AudioStreamWAV` buffers at load: bell partial sets, plucks (Karplus–Strong), flute (sine + breath noise), pads (detuned saw/sine stack with slow filter), noise textures, water, wind. Buffers are cached in `user://cache/audio/` with a version hash; regeneration is skipped if the hash matches.
- `AudioDirector` owns the buses, ambience layers, one-shot pools (16 voices), and music layers. It fails gracefully: if audio initialization fails, the game runs silent and logs once.
- Music scheduling runs on a 1/8-note grid derived from a slow tempo (52–64 BPM), not on frame time, so it stays musical under load.
- No sound plays when the app is backgrounded; the iOS audio session is deactivated on pause to respect other apps.
- All levels are data-driven (`data/audio.json`) so mixing can be tuned without code.
- Accessibility: every non-speech cue that carries gameplay information has a caption and a visual mark; all audio can be muted without losing information.

## 8. Originality and licensing

Every waveform is generated by code written for this project, or (if curated later) is recorded by the team or licensed with a written grant. No copyrighted music, no sample packs without a commercial license, no "inspired by" covers. `docs/ASSET_LICENSES.md` records any exception; currently there are none.
